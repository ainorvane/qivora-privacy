Qivora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · App version: 1.0.0+1

Developer display name: Struggler AI. Support and privacy contact: ai.norvane@gmail.com.

Qivora is a free QR/barcode scanner and QR generator without advertisements or registration. Qivora has no account backend, advertising, profiling or separately integrated analytics/crash-upload service. The Android scanner SDK's diagnostics are described below; this policy does not promise that no data is collected.

Camera, selected photos and QR processing

Camera access is used for scanning. Qivora stops the camera when leaving the visible Scan tab, showing results, selecting an image or entering the background. Qivora does not record or stream video. Images chosen through the system photo picker are analyzed on the device; the entire library is not scanned automatically. Full image metadata is not requested. Qivora does not send image or QR payloads to its own server. In-memory processing is not a secure vault; OS screenshots and recent-app previews follow device behavior.

Temporary picker copies are deleted after processing only within the app's temporary directory. Original photos are not deleted. OS/plugin caches and cleanup after process termination cannot be guaranteed.

Local settings and history

Theme, language, vibration, sound and history preferences are stored in the app's device storage. With history enabled, up to 100 filtered QR web links and checksum-validated EAN/UPC codes are saved with type, source, timestamps, scan count and favorite status. Repeated eligible content from the same source merges. At capacity, the oldest non-favorite is removed; an all-favorite collection rejects new records.

Wi-Fi (including open networks), telephone, email, SMS, contacts, location and free text are excluded from automatic history. Links with query, fragment, user information or likely secret/token paths are excluded entirely; sensitive payloads are not saved as masked values or hashes. A filter cannot prove that every URL is non-personal in every context. You can disable recording or delete records. Disabling recording leaves existing history intact.

Storage is within the OS app sandbox, not an encrypted password vault. Android app rules exclude automatic backup and device transfer; manufacturer behavior still requires device verification. iOS system backups may contain local preferences/history. Qivora operates no cloud account or synchronization service.

Generating, saving and sharing

QR generation happens locally. Forms remain in memory; generated private data is not automatically added to history, apart from eligible filtered web links. Switching type or choosing New QR clears the form. Recognized sensitive content such as Wi-Fi, contacts and messages requires export confirmation; you must also consider whether free text is private.

Choosing Save PNG writes the QR image to Photos/the gallery. Android29+ uses album-free MediaStore without broad media-reading permission; Android24–28 requires legacy write permission. iOS14+ uses add-only access; iOS13 fallback may request full Photos access. The saved image contains the encoded data. Services such as iCloud or Google Photos may synchronize the image according to your settings.

Sharing is initiated by you through the OS share sheet. Qivora removes its temporary PNG after the operation; OS/plugin/recipient copies and delivery are outside Qivora's control. Filenames do not contain QR payloads. Recipient applications have their own policies.

Clipboard and external applications

Copying writes to the clipboard only when you choose it. Wi-Fi passwords are hidden initially and require separate copy confirmation; general Wi-Fi sharing excludes the password. Qivora does not automatically read or later clear your clipboard. OS/keyboards, clipboard synchronization and access by other apps follow device policies.

Every web-link opening displays the full address and actual host and requests confirmation before opening an external app. Risk hints are not a security guarantee. Phone, SMS, email and map buttons open system applications intentionally; Qivora does not automatically send messages or execute arbitrary schemes. External services may use network access and their own data policies.

Third-party components

Android mobile_scanner uses bundled Google ML Kit. Google describes on-device processing of input images and results, while SDK diagnostics/usage metrics may be sent to Google. These may include device/app information, installation identifiers and API performance/event information. Bundling avoids a first-scan model download; it does not imply zero network traffic. See ML Kit privacy, data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

The iOS scanner uses Apple Vision/AVFoundation; Android ML Kit behavior does not automatically apply to iOS. Flutter, intl, image_picker, permission_handler, qr/qr_flutter, gal, path_provider, shared_preferences, share_plus and url_launcher support local/OS operations.

Deletion and control

History and Settings offer clearing with explicit choices to retain favorites or remove all records, followed by confirmation; individual records can also be deleted. Corrupt storage is not reset without explicit consent. Settings are stored separately. Removing app data/uninstalling removes local app storage; manage system backups, saved/shared images, clipboard and recipient copies separately through the relevant services. There is no Qivora account/server profile requiring account deletion. SDK metric retention/deletion is governed by the provider; deleting local history does not delete Google's metrics.

Children and contact

Qivora does not request accounts or age information and has no child-profiling or advertising feature. Camera/QR content can contain personal information; children should use sharing and external links with parental supervision.
